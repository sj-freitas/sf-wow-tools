- **The Attunements tab comes sorted by its first raid, the attuned first**, rather than by name.
- **Sort it another way**: click **Name** above the guildmates for A to Z, again for Z to A, or a
  **raid's icon** above its column for the attuned first, again for the attuned last. The question
  marks stay at the bottom either way, and guildmates the raid reads the same way come in name
  order. One of the two sorts the list at a time, and **Reset** puts it back.
