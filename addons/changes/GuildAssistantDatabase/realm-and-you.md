- **Only the realm you are on.** The Guild and Attunements tabs, what a guildmate can make and who
  can craft a recipe no longer mix in characters from another realm: one of yours with the same
  name there was listed twice, once as "(you)" and once as "(alt)". GuildAssistant Professions
  keeps every character of your account; these lists are of where you are.
- **Your characters are all "(you)"**, whichever one you are playing: "(alt)" is gone, and the name
  says which character a row is. A character's page says "You" for all of them too.
- **Who can craft it is the guild you are in**: a guildmate from a guild you have left is no longer
  listed among the crafters.
