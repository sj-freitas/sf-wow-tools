- **Raid attunements**: each character's attunement is recorded as you log in, enter the world or
  hand a quest in (Onyxia's Lair so far), and shared with your guild like professions. An
  attunement is never taken back, and a character nobody has looked at isn't shown as unattuned.
- The upload carries them to Guild Assistant, and GuildAssistant Database shows who is attuned to
  what in game.
- `/ga-prof status` says how many of your characters are attuned to each raid.
